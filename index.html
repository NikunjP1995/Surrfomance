Built on the inside of Google.

Surrfomance is a performance marketing agency working with product and service businesses across Canada — from single-location clinics to brands selling nationwide.

Our team came out of Google's own digital marketing organisation, supporting advertisers across effectively every category that spends money online. That vantage point shapes how we work: we saw which campaigns compounded, which ones quietly burned budget, and how differently success has to be defined depending on the business behind it.

App companies were judged on install volume. Lead-gen businesses in insurance and mortgage on qualified enquiries. E-commerce on traffic and conversion. CPG on impressions and repeat purchase. Retail on awareness that showed up as foot traffic rather than a click. Agreeing which number counts before anything is built is the step most agencies skip — and the reason so much reporting looks healthy while the business underneath it doesn't move.

We pair that strategy with an in-house build and production capability, so the site, the creative and the campaigns are made by one team rather than handed between three. Scope is fixed before work starts, measurement goes in from day one, and every asset we produce belongs to you.
