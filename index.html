Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026. This policy explains what information Surrfomance collects, why, and how it is handled.

who we are

Surrfomance ("we", "us", "our") is an AI studio that builds AI products, custom automation, and software. This policy covers our website and any direct communication you have with us. If you have questions, contact us through the form on this site.

what we collect

When you submit the project form or book a call, we collect the information you choose to give us — typically your name, email address, company name, project type, and the details you write about your project. We also collect basic, non-identifying technical data through standard web analytics, such as pages visited and general device or browser type. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, and we ask that you do not include any in your messages to us.

how we use it

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare for and schedule a discovery call, and discuss potential work with you. We use aggregate analytics data to understand how the site is used and to improve it. We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

what we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not send marketing emails you did not ask for. We do not share your project details with third parties except where strictly necessary to deliver a service you have requested, or where required by law.

third-party services

We rely on a small number of reputable third-party providers to operate — for example, hosting, form handling, scheduling, and analytics. These providers process limited data on our behalf and are expected to maintain their own appropriate security and privacy standards. Booking a call may take you to a third-party scheduling tool, which will handle your booking information under its own privacy policy.

data retention

We keep enquiry and project information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected — to hold our conversation, deliver any agreed work, and meet legal or accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, contact us through the form on this site and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law.

security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe and limit access to it.

changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices or legal obligations change. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above. We encourage you to review it periodically.
