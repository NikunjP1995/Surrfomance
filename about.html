our story

Built after seven years of watching agencies get it wrong.

Surrfomance started with a simple observation: most digital marketing agencies stop at the click. They celebrate impressions, cost-per-click, and "engagement" — while their clients quietly wonder where the actual customers are.

After seven years running performance marketing for real estate agents, pre-construction developers, and service-based businesses across North America — including a portfolio of 30+ GTA realtors through a major real estate marketing platform — one pattern kept repeating. The agencies that ran the ads were disconnected from the CRM that captured the leads, which was disconnected from the follow-up that closed the deal. Money leaked at every handoff.

Surrfomance was built to close those gaps. One team owns the whole thing — ads, landing pages, CRM, AI follow-up, reporting — so campaigns optimize toward closed deals, not form fills. And so a solo realtor gets the same connected system that a franchise brokerage usually pays six figures for.

what we believe

Six principles
we don't compromise on.

01 · outcomes

Track to revenue, not clicks

Every lead is tagged to its source campaign, keyword, and ad. Closed deals get fed back into ads so campaigns learn what actually earns. Vanity metrics don't pay commissions.

02 · speed

Speed to lead is the game

A lead you respond to in 90 seconds converts at 4× the rate of one you get to in an hour. AI-driven instant follow-up is baked into every engagement we run.

03 · ownership

You own everything

Your ad accounts, website, CRM data, and leads are 100% yours from day one. No proprietary lock-ins. No hostage situations. If we stop working together, nothing breaks.

04 · clarity

Plain-English reporting

Reports focused on what matters: leads generated, calls booked, deals closed, cost per outcome. No jargon dashboards designed to obscure what's actually happening.

05 · niche

Depth over breadth

We only take on service businesses where lead generation is the growth lever — realtors, brokers, firms. Depth in a few verticals beats being a jack-of-all-trades.

06 · leverage

AI as leverage, not gimmick

AI belongs in the boring stuff — instant follow-up, lead qualification, reporting, workflow automation. Strategy and creative still take human judgment. We use both.
